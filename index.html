01 / Research

The early Universe, from faint galaxies to growing black holes.

01.1
First galaxies & reionization
I use ultra-deep JWST imaging and spectroscopy to measure the physical properties of intrinsically faint galaxies in the first billion years, with particular emphasis on star formation, chemical enrichment, nebular emission, and their contribution to cosmic reionization.
01.2
Little Red Dots & black-hole growth
I investigate compact red sources discovered by JWST and the connection between early black-hole growth, AGN activity, and their host galaxies.
01.3
JWST imaging & spectroscopy
My work combines NIRCam, MIRI, NIRSpec, and NIRISS data, from reduction and calibration through multi-wavelength catalogues, spectral analysis, and SED modelling.

03 / About

Observational astrophysics across the UV to the mid-infrared.

Profile
Galaxy formation and evolution
My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution at high redshift, using imaging and spectroscopy from HST and JWST. I work on star-forming galaxies, AGN, Little Red Dots, and the build-up of structure in the early Universe.
Methods
Data → physics
My expertise spans UV-to-IR astronomy, multi-wavelength catalogues, SED fitting, imaging and spectroscopic data reduction, emission-line measurements, morphology, and the interpretation of galaxy physical properties.
